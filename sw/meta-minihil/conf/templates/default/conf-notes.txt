Welcome to the MiniHIL Yocto Project build environment!

Common bitbake targets:
  - core-image-minimal: Minimal bootable console image.
  - core-image-base: Console image with full RPi hardware support.
  - minihil-image: Custom MiniHIL image including custom tools.

To build the MiniHIL image, run:
    bitbake minihil-image

To view available image recipes in the layers:
    bitbake-layers show-recipes "*-image*"
